Disclaimers and Finances

For some events (especially parties, large public events, anything that may be particularly messy) we will ask for a £20 deposit. The coop doesn't have loads of money to go around, especially since the energy price crisis began. Housing is expensive! So we ask for a small fee to cover the PRS music licencing for the space as a venue when music is a part of events. This is rarely higher than £20 for most events featuring music, we will use your deposit to pay the fee then return the remainder to you. Please let us know if you feel unable to pay the fee, as we can lift it to support organisations in need.
